# Migrating away from Camunda 7

Camunda 7 Community Edition reached its end of life with 7.24.0, and the Enterprise Edition is maintained until 2030.
This folder collects one guide per target engine.

| Camunda 8 | [`c8/`](c8/README.md) | Planned |
| CIB seven | [`cib-seven/`](cib-seven/README.md) | Planned |
